About this model

Trained on C-STAR participant data using 5-fold cross-validation with Optuna hyperparameter tuning. Each subscore uses whichever of RandomForest, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, or ElasticNet performed best under cross-validation. Models were converted to ONNX and run entirely client-side via onnxruntime-web — no server-side inference, no network calls after the page loads.

For research and clinical decision-support purposes only. Not a substitute for a full WAB administration or professional clinical judgment.
